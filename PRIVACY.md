# AutoReject Privacy policy

AutoReject Privacy inspects the visible controls and state of supported consent dialogs on HTTP and HTTPS pages. It uses this information locally to decline optional consent. It does not send browsing activity, settings or consent history to the developers or to a telemetry service. The website's own consent manager may process your choice under that website's privacy policy.

The extension keeps your settings and the hostnames you pause on this device. If you enable local history, it also stores up to 200 actions with the site hostname, consent provider, outcome and timestamp. Local history is off for new installations. Existing installations retain their chosen setting. Turning history off stops new entries. The **Wyczyść historię** button deletes stored entries.

The extension reads the active site's address when you open its popup to show the site status and let you pause or resume automation. It stores the hostname when you pause a site. On Cookiebot forms, it reads the consent cookie locally to check whether optional categories were turned off. It does not retain the cookie value.

For privacy questions or support, contact the maintainers through the project repository. [AutoReject Privacy on GitHub](https://github.com/zetH888/AutoReject-Privacy)
